How it works & recent activity
  • Sit where you want the pointer to rest, then press Re-center.
  • Move your head to steer. Sensitivity scales the travel.
  • Pinch thumb and index to click; risky buttons still need a spoken “confirm”.
  • If the cursor drifts after you move, press Re-center again.
  • Gaze modes estimate direction from iris position in the webcam image. They are less precise than head pointing and are not calibrated against your screen.
  • A pinch needs two frames and a short cooldown, so holding it fires one click.
